# Wavee growth plan

*October 2026. Wavee is a Kaivon product at wavee.kaivon.app.*

## The bet

Wavee can't outspend TikTok or out-catalogue Spotify, Audiomack and Boomplay. It wins on one thing: **finding new artists before everyone else.**

Wavee has two sides: artists and listeners. Artists bring their fans, so **recruit artists first**. Then make every song an artist shares bring listeners back to Wavee.

The starting market is Nigeria and its scenes (Afrobeats, Amapiano, Alté, street-hop, gospel, campus artists). Music already spreads there through WhatsApp Status, Instagram and TikTok.

## What's built

| Feature | Why it grows Wavee |
|---|---|
| **Artist links** (Spotify, Apple Music, Audiomack, Boomplay, YouTube, SoundCloud, Deezer, TIDAL, Bandcamp, Instagram, TikTok, X, website) | An artist's Wavee page becomes their **link in bio**. Every artist's Instagram and TikTok bio then sends fans to Wavee for free. |
| **Song links and pre-save** (DistroKid HyperFollow and other smart links) | Artists promote releases through Wavee even when the song's main home is Spotify. |
| **The hook** | Artists mark where the song gets good, and the feed plays from there, like a TikTok sound. Listeners decide faster and keep swiping. |
| **Story cards** | One tap makes a 1080×1920 picture (cover, title, artist, "Listen on Wavee") for WhatsApp Status, Instagram and TikTok stories. Every post is a free ad. |
| **Charts** (Top 50, Rising, Fresh, and a chart per genre) | Fresh content to post every week, plus a reason for artists to share ("I'm #3 on Wavee"). Charts count only real listens: never the artist's own plays, never paid ones. |
| **Tastemakers** (the "Early Ear" badge and leaderboard) | Listeners score when songs they liked early, after really listening, become hits. Digging for new music becomes a game. |
| **Shared-link previews** | Every song and artist link shows its own cover and title in WhatsApp, X and iMessage. |

## The first 90 days

### Weeks 1–2: get ready
- Clear out test uploads of other artists' music. Wavee must launch clean, or it loses its standing with rights holders.
- Set up Instagram, TikTok and X as **@waveemusic** (or similar), a WhatsApp Channel, and a waitlist form for artists.
- Make a 20-second launch video: the feed, a hook playing, a story card being shared.

### Weeks 2–6: the first 100 artists (do it by hand)
- Message artists directly. Start with campus artists, producers, open-mic and showcase regulars in Abuja and Lagos, and gospel and church musicians.
- Use a simple pitch: *"Free page for your music and every platform you're on, a weekly chart to climb, and new listeners from the feed."*
- Help each artist set up: profile photo, links, a hook on every song, and the bio link copied into their Instagram.
- Give the first 100 a **Founding Artist** mark on their profile, and make sure they know they were first.

### Weeks 4–12: weekly habits
- **Monday:** post the chart (Top 10 graphic and a Rising pick) on all socials and tag the artists. Charted artists repost it.
- **Wednesday:** "Fresh Finds", three new songs picked by the team, posted as story cards.
- **Friday:** shout out the top tastemakers. Listeners compete to be named.
- **Monthly:** "Wavee Fresh Finds" contest. The winner gets homepage placement for a week and a small cash or gear prize. Later, sponsors pay for it.

### Partners
- **Campus radio and DJs:** they play the weekly chart and get credit on Wavee.
- **Music blogs and curators:** give them early access to the Rising chart as a source of stories.
- **Showcases and open mics:** a QR code on stage leading to the performing artist's Wavee page.

## Making money (in this order)

1. **Boost (paid promotion).**
   - Artists already pay blogs, DJs and curators to be heard.
   - Boost puts a song in the feed as clearly labelled *Promoted*, to a set number of listeners, with real stats afterwards (how many listened past the hook, liked, followed).
   - Start small and adjust: price per real listener, not per view.
   - **Rule:** boosted plays never count toward charts. That's what makes the charts worth climbing, and Boost worth paying for.
2. **Artist Pro (monthly subscription):**
   - detailed stats (who listens, for how long, where they drop off, which city);
   - pre-save pages for releases, release scheduling and a custom profile look;
   - more and larger uploads.
3. **Tips:** fans support artists directly through Flutterwave, and Wavee keeps a small cut. Supporters get a badge on the artist's page.
4. **Sponsored contests:** telecoms, drinks and fashion brands pay to run "Battle of the Campuses" and similar competitions.
5. **Ads, later.** Ads only pay at large scale, and ad rates in Nigeria are low. They're the last layer, not the business model.
6. **Data for labels:** once Wavee reliably spots rising artists first, labels and A&R teams would pay for that early signal. This could be the most valuable business of all.

*Payments for 1–3 wait on Flutterwave being live for Kaivon.*

## What to measure every week

| Number | Why |
|---|---|
| Artists who uploaded this week | The supply side; everything starts here |
| Listeners who come back within 7 days | Whether the feed is actually good |
| Shares per song (links + story cards) | The growth engine |
| Plays that started from a shared link | Whether sharing brings people in |
| Songs that finish (listened past the hook) | Whether the hook and the feed work |
| Bio links set by artists | Whether Wavee became their link in bio |

Healthy early targets: 100 active artists, 1,000 weekly listeners, and a third of plays coming from shared links.

## What comes next in the product

- **Release notifications:** when someone you follow drops a song, or your song makes a chart (web push and email).
- **Video story cards:** a 15-second clip of the hook with a moving waveform, for TikTok and Reels.
- **A better feed:** rank by how many listeners finish a song, not just plays. This is TikTok's real secret.
- **City charts:** Abuja, Lagos, Port Harcourt and more, from approximate location.
- **Embeddable player:** for blogs and artists' own websites.
- **Mobile apps:** after the web proves people come back.

## Risks to manage

- **Copyright:** one stolen hit can sink Wavee's reputation.
  - Keep the rights checkbox, act on takedowns within two days, and close repeat infringers' accounts.
  - Don't run promotions on music from big-name artists unless it was uploaded by them.
- **Fake plays:** charts already ignore short listens, repeat listens within 30 seconds and the artist's own plays. Watch for bot farms once Boost exists, and remove fake counts.
- **Moderation load:** recruit two or three trusted moderators once uploads pass about 50 a week.
